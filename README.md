## What we can say now

The supplied chart proposes a Kramer line through three successive Ferdinands to Matthew Kramer (born 1840) and then Bernhard Kramer/Kraemer. State indexes match the chart's **24 August 1913 Ferdinand birth** (mother “Bosch R”) and **15 July 1918 Matthias death** in Wilkes-Barre. An original [1940 census sheet](https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/census-1940/T627/PA/m-t0627-03563/m-t0627-03563-00103.jpg) places the older Ferdinand, a wire-rope foreman, with daughter Hilda at 146 Prospect; it records his eighth-grade education, 1939 wages, and an owned home estimated at $2,400. A [1950 sheet](https://1950census.archives.gov/iiif/2/1950census%2F43290879-Pennsylvania%2F43290879-Pennsylvania-135659%2F43290879-Pennsylvania-135659-0014.jpg/full/full/0/default.jpg) finds him and Hilda there again with son-in-law Joseph. A separate [1950 household](https://1950census.archives.gov/iiif/2/1950census%2F43290879-Pennsylvania%2F43290879-Pennsylvania-135713%2F43290879-Pennsylvania-135713-0005.jpg/full/full/0/default.jpg) puts younger Ferdinand, 36, with wife Mary Andrews Kramer and a son Ferdinand, strongly matching Ferdinand Francis's obituary. **The link between the two Ferdinand households, and from the older man back to Matthew, still needs direct evidence.**

The chart's long German branch belongs to **Magdalena Disque**, who married into the Kramer family. A specialist Palatinate mill history independently documents Sebastian Disqué, Anna Barbara Schaaf, and their mill operations. The later chain from the 1700s to Magdalena remains a research target; published accounts and the chart disagree on some dates and spouses.

The Weatherford–Smith branch currently begins with information supplied directly by the family. Common names and a city alone cannot identify earlier generations reliably.

## Evidence rules

**Recorded** means a cited record directly states the fact. **Family-supplied** means the chart or Justin/Ashley gave it. **Proposed** means records fit but identity or relationship still needs proof. **Context** describes a place or era and is never presented as something a named ancestor personally experienced. Dates and spellings are preserved as each source gives them; conflicts stay visible.

The repository is currently private. Avoid adding living people's addresses, contact information, precise birth dates, finances, or unverified claims. No individual wealth figure, school experience, motive for migration, or lost inheritance is established in this pass.
