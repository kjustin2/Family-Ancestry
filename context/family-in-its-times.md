## The migration questions

**When did Rose Bosch Kramer's parents arrive?** The [1920 census continuation](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:33S7-9RFQ-J7P?view=index&personArk=%2Fark%3A%2F61903%2F1%3A1%3AMFBB-JCW&action=view&lang=en) records **Amiel and Hildegard Bosch** as born in **Baden** and reports immigration around **1880**. They were living with Rose's family in Wilkes-Barre by 1920. This is a dated family-report lead, not a passenger manifest; the departure town, route, first U.S. residence and reason for leaving remain unknown.

**Why did the Disques leave?** The [1864 institutional card](https://migration.pfalzgeschichte.de/person/98001) says widow Magdalena Fröhlich Disque moved with four children **after** her husband's death in 1853. That is a household circumstance, not a recorded motive or immediate trigger. The [regional migration history](https://www.auswanderung-rlp.de/ziele-der-auswanderung/auswanderung-nach-nordamerika/ausstellung-zur-auswanderung-2010.html) places their departure at the start of a Palatinate migration peak driven in part by divided farmland, crowded trades and earlier economic crises. A personal explanation would require a **family letter, emigration application, property/asset statement or other firsthand record**. The original passenger list would test who traveled; the card's named children do **not** yet connect the chart's Magdalena to this party. [Economic evidence by generation](../research/assets-by-generation.md).

**Why did the Kramers leave Germany?** We cannot yet establish that **Bernhard or Matthew Kramer** emigrated from Germany at all. The chart gives no birthplace for Matthew, and the paternal Kramer link to Bernhard is unproved. The documented German mill and 1864 migration leads belong to the **Disqué side**. Find Bernhard and Matthew in a birthplace-bearing census, marriage, naturalization, death, or church record before giving the Kramer surname a migration story.

**When did Ashley's ancestors arrive?** The [branch-by-branch answer](../branches/ashley-immigration.md) has no verified immigration date yet. Garnett Weatherford Sr. was born in Virginia around 1926–27; the earlier Weatherford/Neatherly, Morrison and Smith parent chains must be extended before an immigrant can be identified.
