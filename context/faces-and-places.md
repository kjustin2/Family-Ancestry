**A personality clue from someone who knew her:** [Paulene Miller Beach's memoir](https://bhaven.org/paulene/life-journey) remembers her mother **Mabel/Pearl Wilson Miller** with deep affection and describes her cleaning and ironing work, family music, and a later island cruise sent by son Paul. Those are **Paulene's memories**, not a neutral character assessment or proof of the cruise itinerary. The [Miller family page](../branches/miller-gower-reese.md#small-stories-paulene-preserved) preserves the details and the records that can check its dates.

**What to look for next:** A labeled family album, workplace newsletter, school yearbook with a verified name, or a property photograph with a dated address could turn these broad settings into personal stories.
