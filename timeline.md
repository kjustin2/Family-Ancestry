This compares **documented work and assets**, not measured net worth. The German mill branch is one ancestral line among many; its property cannot be treated as wealth inherited by every later descendant.

**Economic reading:** Mill operation indicates access to valuable productive equipment or leases, but neither the asset list nor later records here yields a defensible monetary estimate. The Knittelsheim operation's documented succession to particular sons/brothers is one possible reason the asset did not follow every descendant. There is **no evidence of a family fortune being lost**, nor proof that Magdalena's line owned that mill. Wilkes-Barre directories show several occupations at one address, including women's book and clothing work; they provide no household income or quantified fall in wealth.
