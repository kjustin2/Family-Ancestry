# The family tree: choose a person, then follow the evidence

**Justin Kramer and Ashley Weatherford** connect the four main branches below. Each linked name opens a short research guide; the four diagrams show longer ancestral paths with uncertainty marked on the connecting lines. This is a reading map, not a claim that every older generation has been proved.

## Justin's paternal paths

- [Kramer, Bosch and German candidates](branches/kramer.md): **Paul** ← **Fred / Ferdinand Francis (1939)** ← **Ferdinand Louis (1913)** ← **Ferdinand (c. 1882)** ← **Matthew and Lena**. The [older Unadingen family](branches/kramer-unadingen-household.md) has its own original records; whether its Matthäus is Pennsylvania's Matthew is [unproved](branches/kramer-baden-candidate.md).
- [Andrews and Kennedy](branches/andrews-kennedy.md): Fred's mother **Mary Andrews** ← **Frank Andrews and Mary Kennedy** ← **Edward Kennedy and Mary Durkin**, named as Mary's parents on her [1929 death certificate](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/126529268/mary-andrews/photo#view-photo=218591682). Edward and Mary were Ireland-born and had eight children in the [original 1900 Parsons census](sources/records/edward-mary-kennedy-census-1900-sheet-22a.jpg) and [continuation](sources/records/edward-mary-kennedy-census-1900-sheet-22b.jpg); daughter Mary's birth-year estimates conflict. The [1920 Andrews census](sources/records/frank-andrews-family-census-1920-sheet-3a.jpg), [original 1929 notice](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/126529268/mary-andrews/photo#view-photo=218591721), and [1930 census](sources/records/frank-andrews-children-census-1930.jpg) trace younger Mary and sister Rita. [Household-change visual](maps/andrews-1920-1930-household.svg) · [later Flick Street visual](maps/andrews-flick-households.svg).
- [Cordaro and Caucci](branches/cordaro-passport-lead.md): **Patricia Cordaro** ← **Joseph Cordaro and Dina Caucci**. Joseph's documented Italian side runs through **Antonio Cordaro and Pietra Magro** toward [Sutera, Sicily](maps/cordaro-sutera-places.svg). His sister **Calogera**, very strongly identified with **Fannie Cordaro Mattina** by parents and an exact 1909 arrival day, has a [five-stop life and home visual](maps/calogera-fannie-journey-1900-1952.svg). Dina's [Caucci–Belardinelli path](branches/caucci-belardinelli.md) has a strong Sassoferrato, Marche, candidate with an open parent bridge.
- [See the paternal tree diagram](maps/full-tree-paternal-kramer-cordaro.svg) and [paternal storyboards](context/family-storyboards.md).

## Justin's maternal paths

- [Miller, Gower and Reese](branches/miller-gower-reese.md): **Melissa** ← **Paul Miller** ← **Lester Miller and Velma Wilson** ← the older **Amos and Mary Miller** household. The [Miller timeline](branches/miller-berwick-timeline.md) pairs Berwick work with Paulene's stories. Earlier Reese/Wilson identities require a [separate check](branches/miller-gower-reese.md).
- [Raber and Kreischer](branches/raber-kreischer-balliet-record-trail.md): **Melissa** ← **Sonya Raber** ← **William John Raber and Shirley Kreischer**. William John's application index names **William G. Raber and Alice L. Houser**; William's [signed card](https://www.myheritage.com/research/record-20912-3208760/william-glenmore-raber-in-united-states-world-war-ii-draft-registrations) identifies him as **Glenmore** and names **Mary Rebecca Raber** as his mother. This strongly joins the older Raber–Shadle household, though **William A.** as Glenmore's father still needs an original parent-naming record. Shirley's parents were **William H. Kreischer and Alethea Sponenberg**. The [older Kreischer and Sponenberg guide](branches/raber-kreisher-fairchild.md) reaches John Kreischer through a retrospective biography, with the father-to-husband William identity marked as a gap.
- [Balliet and Rinehimer](branches/balliet-raber-candidates.md): Sonya married **Floyd Balliet**. Family identification connects him to **Stanley Balliet and Crystal Rinehimer**; the census trail goes back through **Nelson** to probable parents **Michael and Catherine Balliet**. The adult Floyd-to-child link and the 1870 parent inference remain visible in the [record trail](branches/raber-kreischer-balliet-record-trail.md).
- [See the maternal tree diagram](maps/full-tree-maternal-miller-sponenberg.svg) and [three-line Raber–Kreischer–Balliet visual](maps/raber-kreischer-balliet-record-trail.svg).

## Ashley's paternal paths

- [Weatherford, Neathery and Phelps](branches/weatherford-smith-generations.md): **Ashley** ← **John Sr.** ← **Garnett Sr. and Florence** ← **Doctor Duffy Weatherford and Annie Neathery**. Duffy's certificate names **George C. Weatherford and Ella Lumpkin**; Annie's names **John R. Neathery and Annie Phelps**. The [Samuel and Jane Weatherford extension](branches/weatherford-early-virginia.md) is a strong earlier candidate with an Asa/Thomas and Ann/Julia name conflict. Two [Halifax court cases](branches/weatherford-early-virginia.md#julia-oakess-older-family-two-court-files) now name **Julia Oakes Weatherford's father Edward** and **her maternal grandfather William Adams**.
- [Morrison and Hollinger](branches/morrison-hollinger.md): **Florence Morrison Weatherford** ← **Roscoe Morrison and Eleanor Hollinger**. Thomas and Isophena Morrison are probable older relatives through Roscoe's mother Celia/Delia; his original birth record is still needed.
- [See the paternal tree diagram](maps/full-tree-weatherford-morrison.svg).

## Ashley's maternal paths

- [Smith, Mulhall and Corbett](branches/smith-mulhall-corbett.md): **Alice Smith Weatherford** ← **James Allen Smith and Gladys Blevins**. A highly likely, still unclosed match takes James to **Raymond J. Smith and Alice Mulhall**, then to **John T. Mulhall and Margaret Corbett**. John's parents **John and Mary Mulhall** were born in Ireland; the [Irish county remains unknown](branches/smith-mulhall-corbett.md#where-in-ireland).
- [Blevins, Hines, Cowan and Mason](branches/blevins-deep-lineage.md): **Gladys Blevins** ← **William Howard Blevins and Mary Hines**. Mary's documented parents are [John Hines and Nora Cowan](branches/smith.md); the [Cowan–Mason records](branches/cowan-mason.md) extend her side. **William Elkanah Blevins ← Shubiel and Ada** is documented, but the **William Howard ← William Elkanah** link remains a household lead. [Colonial Blevins lines](branches/blevins-colonial-james.md) are separate hypotheses until the missing steps are found.
- [See the maternal tree diagram](maps/full-tree-smith-blevins.svg) and [Ashley's immigration status](branches/ashley-immigration.md).
