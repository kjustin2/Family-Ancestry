## Geographic map

The [family burial locator](family-burial-places.svg) marks cemetery regions from Pennsylvania to southwest Virginia. The [Berwick close-up](berwick-family-cemeteries.svg) separates Roselawn, Elan, Pine Grove, and Briar Creek Union on an approximate coordinate plot; clickable numbered dots lead to cemetery pages. Filled dots have a funeral program or obituary for at least one family burial, while outlined dots are contributor-memorial leads. Neither map marks a grave. [Person-by-person burial evidence and visit checklist](../context/family-burial-places.md).

[Immigration evidence windows](immigration-evidence-windows.svg) compares five crossings or crossing leads on one date scale. It distinguishes a **Mulhall child-birthplace bracket**, reported Kramer arrival, Corbett candidate deadline, Disqué migration card and Pietra Cordaro's manifest. This is a dated evidence diagram; the [immigration guide](../context/immigration-stories.md) supplies the citations and the maps below supply geographic context.

The [family geography atlas](../context/geography-atlas.md) combines three scale-specific maps: [European origins](europe-family-places.svg), [southwest Germany](germany-family-places.svg), and [eastern U.S. chapters](america-family-places.svg). The Europe map now separates **Sassoferrato, Marche** on Joseph Caucci's [1942 card](../sources/records/joseph-caucci-draft-card-1942.jpg) from the Cordaro family's **Sutera, Sicily**. Each map also has a PNG preview. Run `python maps/generate_geography_atlas.py` to regenerate them; it reads [auditable OSM place centers](place-centers.json) and downloads [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/) outlines to the ignored `tmp/` cache. The coordinates are approximate place centers credited to [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) under ODbL. Modern borders and grouped centers are for orientation, not proof of historical residence or travel.

[Louisa Straub's France/Germany birthplace reports](straub-origin-evidence.svg) ([PNG preview](straub-origin-evidence-preview.png)) color the **two country labels in her records** without inventing a birth town. Her [original 1941 Pennsylvania certificate](../sources/records/louisa-straub-hollinger-death-certificate-1941.jpg) reports France for her and father Nicholas; 1900–30 censuses say Germany. Modern country outlines are context, not a claim about the 1877 border. Regenerate with `python maps/generate_straub_origin_map.py` (Natural Earth 1:110m, public domain).

[Family place sequences](family-place-sequences.svg) uses approximate town-center positions in Pennsylvania and Virginia to show the **Muncy Valley → Berwick → Muncy** maternal return, plus paternal and Weatherford settings. Dashed connections cross generations and have **unknown move dates**; no living home is pinned. [Sources and county-name caveat](../context/family-place-sequences.md).

[Sutera and Milocca in Italy and Sicily](cordaro-sutera-places.svg) ([PNG preview](cordaro-sutera-places-preview.png)) marks the passport's Sutera, Antonio's **1878 Milocca birth and 1899 marriage**, Pietra's Sutera last home, and **Palermo as her documented 1909 departure port**. It notes the local 1905 landslide and mine losses as context, with no claimed Cordaro motive. Milocca was then a Sutera hamlet and is now Milena; Caltanissetta city is orientation only. The portrait location and Antonio's exact Atlantic crossing remain unknown. Regenerate it with `python maps/generate_sutera_map.py` (Natural Earth 1:50m outline; approximate OpenStreetMap place centers), then see the [Cordaro evidence](../branches/cordaro-passport-lead.md).

[Open the generated SVG map](ancestral-places.svg) or [PNG preview](ancestral-places-preview.png). It locates regions and town centers, not homes. **Berwick and Wilkes-Barre share a northeast Pennsylvania marker at the nationwide scale**, while the Palatinate inset uses approximate coordinates so nearby mill villages remain legible. The German locations are independently recorded mill places; the proposed link from their families to Magdalena Disque in Wilkes-Barre is still under investigation.

The map was made with [generate_map.py](generate_map.py) from [Natural Earth 1:110m country outlines](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/) (public domain) and approximate town-center coordinates. Run `python maps/generate_map.py` to regenerate the SVG; this requires a network connection for the outline GeoJSON. The optional Python `cairosvg` package also regenerates the PNG preview. No map tile or geocoding service is needed to view either map.

For street-level historic maps, use [Alexandria's historic-map collection](https://alexandriava.gov/historic-alexandria/basic-page/historic-alexandria-maps) and [Luzerne County's map and directory resources](https://luzerne.pagenweb.org/). Current streets and buildings must not be assumed identical to those in an ancestor's time.
