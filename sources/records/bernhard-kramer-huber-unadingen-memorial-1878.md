# Bernhard Kramer and Maria Huber: the 1878 Unadingen memorial gift

<a href="bernhard-kramer-huber-unadingen-memorial-1878.jpg"><img src="bernhard-kramer-huber-unadingen-memorial-1878.jpg" width="480" alt="1878 Freiburg archdiocesan bulletin page, with item 113 near the lower left"></a>

*Click the page for the full-size image; item 113 is near the lower left.*

**Source:** Freiburg archdiocesan *Anzeigeblatt* (1878), printed page **24**, item **113**; [official issue, PDF page 6](https://www.kirchenrecht-ebfr.de/kabl/7963.pdf#page=6). The saved page image is above. This is a contemporary **published gift notice**, not a gravestone inscription, death certificate or list of the children.

## What the notice says

> **German, transcribed:** “113. In den Kirchenfond Unadingen: von den Kindern des † Bernhard Kramer und der Maria geb. Huber zu einer Jahrtagsmesse für ihre Eltern 100 M.”

> **English:** “113. To the Unadingen church fund: from the children of the late Bernhard Kramer and Maria, née Huber, 100 marks for an annual memorial Mass for their parents.”

The cross (**†**) is printed before **Bernhard** only. It directly marks him as deceased when the notice was published. The notice does not give either parent's death date or explicitly state whether Maria was then alive. *Geb.* abbreviates **geborene**, “born” or “née.” *Kirchenfond* is the parish church fund; *Jahrtagsmesse* is a Mass held each year in remembrance. [Freiburg's present-day description of a *Messstiftung*](https://www.kirchenrecht-ebfr.de/document/10054) explains the general model of a gift whose proceeds support Masses; it does not supply the terms of this 1878 gift. The **100 marks** are the reported contribution, **not** a statement that 100 marks were paid every year.

## What we learn about this household

- At least two children acted together, or were credited together, to arrange a recurring Catholic remembrance for Bernhard and Maria in **Unadingen**. The notice does not name them or say where they lived in 1878.
- Bernhard had died by publication. A separate [Unadingen burial index](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:WFDG-3LT2?lang=en) reports **1 March 1874** as his burial date; the bulletin itself cannot verify that date. A [Maria Huber burial candidate](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:WFD2-GHPZ?lang=en) reports **10 September 1875**, but does not name a spouse.
- The gift shows a specific act of family commemoration and a connection to the local parish. It does **not** measure Bernhard and Maria's estate, show which child paid, or prove that the child Matthäus in the [1840 Unadingen register](unadingen-matthaeus-kramer-register-1840-candidate.jpg) was the later Matthew Kramer in Pennsylvania.
