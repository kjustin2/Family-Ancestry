# The family-held Italian passport: visible-page translation

*Click the page for the full-size handwriting.*

This translation covers **only the photographed inside page and cover**. Justin supplied the photographs; their holder's place in the Cordaro family is not yet proved. [See the identity comparison](../../branches/cordaro-passport-lead.md).

| Italian on the document | English reading | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Regno d'Italia — Passaporto per l'Estero* (cover) | **Kingdom of Italy — passport for travel abroad.** | A passport is permission/documentation for travel, not proof that a voyage occurred. |
| *Il presente passaporto consta di venti pagine* | **This passport contains twenty pages.** | Only two pages have been photographed here; visa and travel stamps on the others might date a journey. |
| *N. del Passaporto* | **Passport number**, written approximately **3015**. | The last digits should be checked against another page or register. |
| *In nome di Sua Maestà Vittorio Emanuele III, per grazia di Dio e per volontà della nazione, Re d'Italia* | **In the name of His Majesty Victor Emmanuel III, by the grace of God and the will of the nation, King of Italy.** | Printed state formula, not a personal connection to the king. |
| *rilasciato a* [name] | **Issued to** a holder whose handwriting appears to read **Onofrio Cordaro**. | **Provisional name reading**; it must be tested against the original Sutera civil register. Do not silently replace it with the different documented Antonio Cordaro. |
| *figlio di Giuseppe e di* [Maria Tona / Tona Maria] | **Son of Giuseppe and Maria Tona**, if the cursive name reading is right. | The mother's name order and exact letter forms are uncertain. This field alone does not identify the holder as Antonio's brother. |
| *nato a Sutera il 13 gennaio 1878* | **Born in Sutera on 13 January 1878.** | The handwritten day and year should be compared with a birth act. |
| *residente a Sutera in provincia di Caltanissetta* | **Resident in Sutera, province of Caltanissetta.** | Residence at issue is not a destination or an emigration date; the issue date is not visible here. |
| *di condizione contadino* | **Occupation/status: farm worker or farmer.** | *Contadino* does not say whether he owned land, what he earned, or why he may have traveled. |

**The useful lead:** the birthplace, date and parent names give a specific person to test in Sutera's birth records. The [separate 1878 Milocca act for Antonio](https://antenati.cultura.gov.it/ark:/12657/an_ua18544377/) and [Antonio's later U.S. petition](../../sources/records/antonio-cordaro-petition-1927.jpg) document *his* line, but do not yet identify this passport's bearer or any voyage on it.
