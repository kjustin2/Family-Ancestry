# Weatherford and Smith: generations and siblings

![Two family lines meeting at John and Alice, with known sibling counts](../maps/weatherford-smith-generations.svg)

This is the **known family structure**, not a complete legal pedigree. “Unknown” means no reliable date or relative was found in the sources reviewed. A person listed as surviving in an old obituary was alive **then**; that does not establish present status. Living relatives' exact birth dates are left unfilled unless a family record is supplied. ## Layer 2 — Garnett's generation and the Miller Drive parents

**Garnett Burnell Weatherford Sr.** was born in **Danville, Virginia, about 1926–27** (inferred from his obituary's age 80) and died **27 June 2007**. He married **Florence Ruth Morrison**; a Fairfax index is dated **16 January 1954** but calls the groom “Garrett.” Florence was alive in Garnett's 2007 obituary; her birth and death dates, parents, and siblings are not established. [Obituary](https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/local/2007/07/03/obituaries/43ba782a-181c-4c59-a78e-7d361d86bf4a/) · [marriage index](https://www.fairfaxcounty.gov/circuit/sites/circuit/files/assets/documents/pdf/hrc/marriage-book-index-1853-1957-brides.pdf).

Helen's obituary identifies **12 siblings**, including Garnett. Birth order is mostly unknown. Her “survived by” and “predeceased by” wording gives the status **as of 12 March 2007**, not necessarily a present-day status. [Helen's obituary](https://www.legacy.com/us/obituaries/godanriver/name/helen-barksdale-obituary?id=33333924).

The [same unsourced compilation](https://cottagehill.com/southside/f544.htm) supplies Thelma's and Lillian's proposed dates. Their obituary listing confirms only that Thelma was alive and Lillian had died by March 2007. Do not attach precise dates to the other names without distinguishing records.

**George's wartime and bus-work lead.** A [separate family-history book](https://www.waltwarnickwesternmarylandfamilyhistory.com/lee-family-book-1) describes George Ira Weatherford as a son of D.D. and Annie, an **AB&W/Metro bus operator for more than 38 years**, and an **Army veteran of 1944–46** with the **84th Infantry Division, 335th Infantry** at the Battle of the Bulge. It reports a **Bronze Star**. If matched to Helen's brother George by an original record, this would put two brothers in the same bus industry: Garnett also worked for AB&W and Metro. The book supplies no visible service citation or employment file, so the details remain a well-defined lead. George's reported **Army** service is separate from Garnett's reported **Navy** service. [Garnett profile](https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/local/2007/07/03/obituaries/43ba782a-181c-4c59-a78e-7d361d86bf4a/).

**James Allen Smith and Gladys Louise Blevins** are named as Alice's parents on her [1986 marriage return](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-C9TY-595M-1?view=index&personArk=%2Fark%3A%2F61903%2F1%3A1%3AQK9V-J612&action=view&cc=2370234&lang=en). The [1976 return for Jane Ann Smith](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-C9TX-59CG-9?view=index&personArk=%2Fark%3A%2F61903%2F1%3A1%3AQVY5-GZKP&action=view&cc=2370234&lang=en) and [1983 return for James Howard Smith](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-C9TT-NHKX-W?view=index&personArk=%2Fark%3A%2F61903%2F1%3A1%3AQV13-2NVL&action=view&cc=2370234&lang=en) independently name the same pair; the last spells her given name **“Glaydis.”** The family recalled “Gladus.” Fairfax County names **Gladys L. Smith heirs** in the 1990 title history at **6330 Miller Drive**. Birth and death dates, parents and sibling groups for James and Gladys are not securely established. The family recalls that James died in the **1990s**; the 1990 title wording is a lead toward an estate, not a death certificate. ## Layer 4 — Ashley's generation

John and Alice's children identified by the family are **Ashley Marie Weatherford**, **John Weatherford Jr.**, and **Tancy Weatherford**. Justin says **Tancy was named for a great-aunt Tancy on Alice's side**. He does not yet know whether the older Tancy belonged to James Smith's or Gladys Blevins's branch, so she is not placed in an earlier sibling list. Birth and death dates and birth order have not been supplied or independently established. Ashley is Justin Kramer's wife. [Family-supplied]
