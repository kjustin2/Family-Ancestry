# Weatherford and Smith: generations and siblings

![Two family lines meeting at John and Alice, with known sibling counts](../maps/weatherford-smith-generations.svg)

This is the **known family structure**, not a complete legal pedigree. The diagram above summarizes where the lines meet; the [deeper Weatherford diagram](../maps/weatherford-deep-lineage.svg) shows the newly documented earlier generations. “Unknown” means no reliable date or relative was found in the sources reviewed. A person listed as surviving in an old obituary was alive **then**; that does not establish present status. Living relatives' exact birth dates are left unfilled unless a family record is supplied. ## Earlier Weatherford and Neathery layer

Duffy's original [1966 death certificate](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-C95D-9HD1?view=index&personArk=%2Fark%3A%2F61903%2F1%3A1%3AQVRZ-2C68&action=view&lang=en) names his parents **George C. Weatherford and Ella Lumpkin**. Annie's original [1969 certificate](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-C95D-49MW-W?view=index&personArk=%2Fark%3A%2F61903%2F1%3A1%3AQVYL-N4BR&action=view&lang=en) names **John R. Neathery and Annie Phelps**. These are **Ashley's great-great-grandparents** through Garnett. Their own birth/death dates are not confirmed here. George and Ella's [1884 marriage register](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QS7-99LX-MC9V?view=index&personArk=%2Fark%3A%2F61903%2F1%3A1%3A6NJ1-VGMH&action=view&lang=en) gives George's parents as **Thomas A. and Ann Weatherford** and Ella's as **Fayette and Mina Lumpkins**. However, a [candidate 1880 household](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:MC51-ZRG?lang=en) with a George C. of the right age indexes his parents as **A.T. and Julia A.**; do not merge these earlier names until the originals and identities are reconciled.

## Layer 2 — Garnett's generation and the Miller Drive parents

**Garnett Burnell Weatherford Sr.** gave **8 March 1927** and **Pittsylvania County, Virginia**, as his birth date and place on a [1946 draft card](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSZX-KJ56?view=index&personArk=%2Fark%3A%2F61903%2F1%3A1%3AQ2W5-YQRZ&action=view&lang=en); his obituary instead says **Danville**. He died **27 June 2007**. He married **Florence Ruth Morrison**; a Fairfax index is dated **16 January 1954** but calls the groom “Garrett.” Florence was alive in Garnett's 2007 obituary; her birth and death dates, parents, and siblings are not established. [Obituary](https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/local/2007/07/03/obituaries/43ba782a-181c-4c59-a78e-7d361d86bf4a/) · [marriage index](https://www.fairfaxcounty.gov/circuit/sites/circuit/files/assets/documents/pdf/hrc/marriage-book-index-1853-1957-brides.pdf).

Helen's obituary identifies **12 siblings**, including Garnett. Birth order is mostly unknown. Her “survived by” and “predeceased by” wording gives the status **as of 12 March 2007**, not necessarily a present-day status. [Helen's obituary](https://www.legacy.com/us/obituaries/godanriver/name/helen-barksdale-obituary?id=33333924).

The [same unsourced compilation](https://cottagehill.com/southside/f544.htm) supplies Thelma's and Lillian's proposed dates. Their obituary listing confirms only that Thelma was alive and Lillian had died by March 2007. Do not attach precise dates to the other names without distinguishing records.

**George's wartime and bus-work lead.** A [separate family-history book](https://www.waltwarnickwesternmarylandfamilyhistory.com/lee-family-book-1) describes George Ira Weatherford as a son of D.D. and Annie, an **AB&W/Metro bus operator for more than 38 years**, and an **Army veteran of 1944–46** with the **84th Infantry Division, 335th Infantry** at the Battle of the Bulge. It reports a **Bronze Star**. If matched to Helen's brother George by an original record, this would put two brothers in the same bus industry: Garnett also worked for AB&W and Metro. The book supplies no visible service citation or employment file, so the details remain a well-defined lead. George's reported **Army** service is separate from Garnett's reported **Navy** service. [Garnett profile](https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/local/2007/07/03/obituaries/43ba782a-181c-4c59-a78e-7d361d86bf4a/).

**James Allen Smith and Gladys Louise Blevins** are named as Alice's parents on her [1986 marriage return](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-C9TY-595M-1?view=index&personArk=%2Fark%3A%2F61903%2F1%3A1%3AQK9V-J612&action=view&cc=2370234&lang=en). The [1976 return for Jane Ann Smith](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-C9TX-59CG-9?view=index&personArk=%2Fark%3A%2F61903%2F1%3A1%3AQVY5-GZKP&action=view&cc=2370234&lang=en) and [1983 return for James Howard Smith](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-C9TT-NHKX-W?view=index&personArk=%2Fark%3A%2F61903%2F1%3A1%3AQV13-2NVL&action=view&cc=2370234&lang=en) independently name the same pair; the last spells her given name **“Glaydis.”** The family recalled “Gladus.” Fairfax County names **Gladys L. Smith heirs** in the 1990 title history at **6330 Miller Drive**. Birth and death dates, parents and sibling groups for James and Gladys are not securely established. The family recalls that James died in the **1990s**; the 1990 title wording is a lead toward an estate, not a death certificate. [County parcel lookup](https://icare.fairfaxcounty.gov/ffxcare/search/commonsearch.aspx?mode=address) · family account.
