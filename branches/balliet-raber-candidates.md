## The Balliet household in an original census

<a href="../sources/records/crystal-balliet-household-census-1940.jpg"><img src="../sources/records/crystal-balliet-household-census-1940.jpg" width="550" alt="Original 1940 Dorrance Township census sheet showing Crystal Balliet and eleven children"></a>

The [original 1940 Dorrance Township census, sheet 6B, lines 49–60](../sources/records/crystal-balliet-household-census-1940.jpg) puts **Crystal Balliet, 40, widowed**, at the head of a **rented home ($10 monthly)**. It explicitly calls the eleven people below her sons or daughters. Justin identifies this as **Sonya's husband Floyd's family**. The original independently documents Crystal's household; it does not carry Floyd forward to his adult name and spouse.

| Age in April 1940 | Children in Crystal's home |
| --- | --- |
| 19–13 | **Arthur 19**, **Luther 18**, **Alice 16**, **Harriet 14**, **Stanley 13** |
| 11–7 | **Lenna 11**, **Ethel 9**, **Margaret 7** |
| 3–1 | **Donald 3**, **Dolores 3**, **Floyd 1** |

Donald’s [2020 obituary](https://www.murphyfuneralservices.com/obituaries/donald-balliet) calls Dolores his **twin**, consistent with their equal census ages, and names **Olive** as another sibling; Olive was **not in this 1940 home**. Thus the two sources name twelve children across the family, eleven at home in 1940. The census has Arthur working as a **farm laborer** and reporting **$48 in 1939 wages for 30 weeks of work**. Crystal reported income from a source other than wages, but the schedule gives no amount. The $48 is **Arthur's reported cash wages**, not household income, property or net worth. No individual Duplan job appears on this sheet.

The [Stairville Cemetery transcription](https://pagenweb.org/~luzerne/township/cemetery/stairville.htm) gives **Stanley D. Balliet's death year as 1937**. A [transcription of the 27 December 1937 *Wilkes-Barre Record* death index](https://genealogytrails.com/penn/luzerne/1937_deaths/a_b_c.html) gives **25 December 1937** for **Stanley Balliet of Stairville**, dying at **Nanticoke State Hospital**; the original newspaper item and death certificate remain to be inspected. Crystal is widowed by the April 1940 census, and her son Floyd is recorded as **one year old**. That age places his birth roughly **April 1938–March 1939**. **Justin identifies Stanley as Floyd's father**, making Floyd a likely **posthumous son** if the newspaper date is accurate. A birth or parent-naming record would independently check that relationship; the census's “son” identifies Floyd's relationship to **head Crystal**, not to Stanley. No personal account of the death has been found.

## What the earlier notices add

Donald’s obituary names siblings **Olive, Lenna, Alice, Luther, Arthur, Stanley Jr., Harriet, Margaret, Ethel, Dolores, and Floyd**. These are *Donald’s* eleven siblings in a published family notice and fit Justin's account of Floyd's siblings. Donald was born in **Albert, Luzerne County, in 1936**; the same notice names Stanley and Crystal Rinehimer as his parents. The cemetery transcription lists **Stanley D. Balliet (1898–1937)** and **Crystal M. Balliet (1899–1973)** in the same section and row, plus Stanley D. Jr. Its death year and the 1940 widow status are compatible; it does not name Floyd's parents directly.

Justin also remembers **Balliet sisters working at the Duplan mill** and a **Debbie Balliet genealogy list**. The sisters' names and dates, Debbie's surname at publication, and the list itself have not been located. [Federal Register entries](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-1941-11-20/pdf/FR-1941-11-20.pdf) put Duplan at **11th and Mulberry Streets, Berwick** in 1941. The mill is a real local setting; it does not by itself identify which Balliets worked there. [Berwick neighborhood sketch](../maps/berwick-raber-kreischer-places.svg).

## Next original records

2. Find **Floyd's birth or parent-naming record** to independently test the posthumous-son account, and inspect Stanley's **27 December 1937 newspaper item/death certificate** to verify the death-index transcription and learn his occupation and cause. Read the **1930 household**, then later marriage records. Seek the reported Duplan jobs in occupational columns, city directories, or mill payrolls.
3. Obtain **William John Raber's original Social Security application/NUMIDENT entry** and Shirley's marriage return. Check his father **William G.'s full middle name**, mother Alice's maiden surname, and possible brother Carl. The 1950 census already names William John as Sonya's father.
4. Locate the reported Debbie Balliet compilation and identify its compiler, sources and date before using its older lineage as fact.
