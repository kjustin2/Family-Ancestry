## The candidate Balliet household

Donald’s obituary names siblings **Olive, Lenna, Alice, Luther, Arthur, Stanley Jr., Harriet, Margaret, Ethel, Dolores, and Floyd**. These are *Donald’s* eleven siblings in a published family notice. They become Floyd A.'s siblings only if the proposed identity bridge is verified. Donald was born in **Albert, Luzerne County, in 1936**; the same notice says their father was Stanley and their mother Crystal. A [Stairville Cemetery transcription](https://pagenweb.org/~luzerne/township/cemetery/stairville.htm) lists **Stanley D. Balliet (1898–1937)** and **Crystal M. Balliet (1899–1973)** in the same section and row, plus Stanley D. Jr. This appears to fit the obituary household, but a marker transcription alone does not prove Crystal's maiden name or Stanley Jr.'s precise parentage. Stanley's reported **1937 death** suggests the youngest children grew up after losing their father; that is a possible family story to test with the 1940 household, not a claim about their individual experience.

Justin also remembers **Balliet sisters working at the Duplan mill** and a **Debbie Balliet genealogy list**. The sisters' names and dates, Debbie's surname at publication, and the list itself have not been located. [Federal Register entries](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-1941-11-20/pdf/FR-1941-11-20.pdf) put Duplan at **11th and Mulberry Streets, Berwick** in 1941. The mill is a real local setting; it does not by itself identify which Balliets worked there. [Berwick neighborhood sketch](../maps/berwick-raber-kreischer-places.svg).

## Next original records

1. Find a marriage record or obituary that names **Floyd A. Balliet with Sonya and parent Stanley or Crystal**. Until then, keep the eleven-sibling group as a candidate.
2. Read the **1940 Stanley/Crystal Balliet household** original (Stanley may already have died), then the children's birth or marriage records. Seek the reported Duplan jobs in occupational columns, city directories, or mill payrolls.
3. Obtain **William John Raber's Social Security application/NUMIDENT entry** and Shirley's marriage return. Check his father **William G.'s full middle name**, mother Alice's maiden surname, and possible brother Carl. Seek a record naming Sonya's father directly.
4. Locate the reported Debbie Balliet compilation and identify its compiler, sources and date before using its older lineage as fact.
