## Sonya's later family

Justin says **Sonya** now lives in **Mountain Top, Pennsylvania**, with husband **Floyd Balliet**. He estimates that they have been married **about 30 years**; no marriage record or exact year has been checked. Justin names their daughter **Raegan Balliet**, who married **Robert Knight**, and Raegan's son **Robert Knight Jr.** The account makes Melissa and Raegan daughters of the same mother through different partners. It does not establish birth dates, wedding dates, occupations, or when Sonya moved to Mountain Top. [Family account in source log](../research/sources.md).

A [2016 Luzerne County Clean and Green tax listing](https://www.luzernecounty.org/DocumentCenter/View/1684/Clean-and-Green-Program-Information-Attachment-3-of-3-PDF) names **Floyd A. and Sonya L. Balliet** together on a **17-acre parcel** with a **$163,800 assessed value**. This independently places the pair on a county property record at that date. Assessment is neither sale price nor household net worth; the listing does not prove a marriage date, their present ownership, or the relationships to Melissa and Raegan. The street address is omitted here because the county-wide parcel location is enough for this history.

Justin places the wider maternal family around **Berwick, Pennsylvania**. He recalls **Aunt Alice, Uncle Bill, Aunt Wendy, Uncle Tim and Uncle Tom** as belonging to **Sonya's side**. The names include siblings and relatives by marriage, but which are Sonya's blood siblings has not yet been specified. Preserve the group without assigning a parent or a surname to each person.

Paulene Miller Beach's [memoir](https://bhaven.org/paulene/life-journey) mentions a **Fairchild dairy** near the *Miller* home. A shared surname and town do not show that its owners were Shirley's Fairchild relatives. First identify Shirley's mother in a record before connecting the dairy.
