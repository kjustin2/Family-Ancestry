# Justin's maternal side: a Berwick guide

![Two Berwick branches joining at Paul Miller and Sonya Raber, then Melissa Miller Kramer](../maps/miller-raber-family.svg)

Justin names **Paul Miller** and **Sonya Raber** as his mother **Melissa Miller Kramer's** parents. Melissa married **Paul Joseph Kramer**; Justin says she worked in Pennsylvania real estate in the 2000s. The [Miller page](miller-gower-reese.md) follows Paul's Berwick ancestors; the [Raber page](raber-kreisher-fairchild.md) holds Sonya's line and the still unnamed Fairchild ancestor. These recent links are **family supplied**. A published account by Paul's sister Paulene gives unusually rich detail on the older Miller household, but its dates and property story still need original records.

| Branch | What we can place | Main gap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Miller → Gower / Reese / Corderman** | Paulene Miller Beach names her brother Paul, parents Lester Miller and Mabel/Pearl Reese Wilson, and earlier relatives. Her sibling list closely matches Justin's. [Read the account](https://bhaven.org/paulene/life-journey). | Birth, marriage, census and deed records to test the memoir's dates and kinship. |
| **Raber → Kreisher → Fairchild** | Justin identifies Sonya's mother as **Shirley Kreisher Raber** and Shirley's mother as a woman born **Fairchild**. | Shirley's mother's given and married names; Sonya's father; dated records joining these generations. |

The family's long Berwick connection is well supported for the **Miller household** by Paulene's first-person account. The Raber/Kreisher/Fairchild places are presently a family memory; each person still needs a record before we assign a particular address or job.
