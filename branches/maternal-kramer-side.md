# Justin's maternal side: a Berwick guide

![Two Berwick branches joining at Paul Miller and Sonya Raber, then Melissa Miller Kramer](../maps/miller-raber-family.svg)

Justin names **Paul Miller** and **Sonya Raber** as his mother **Melissa Miller Kramer's** parents. Melissa married **Paul Joseph Kramer**; Justin says she worked in Pennsylvania real estate in the 2000s. The [Miller page](miller-gower-reese.md) follows Paul's Berwick ancestors; the [Raber page](raber-kreisher-fairchild.md) now follows Sonya through Shirley, Aletha and the Sponenbergs. Melissa's recent parent links are **family supplied**; the older Sonya–Shirley link appears in a published obituary. A published account by Paul's sister Paulene gives unusually rich detail on the older Miller household, but its dates and property story still need original records.

Justin also reports that Sonya has been married to **Floyd Balliet** for about 30 years and now lives with him in **Mountain Top, Pennsylvania**. Their daughter **Raegan Balliet** married **Robert Knight** and has a son, **Robert Knight Jr.** The [Sonya family diagram](../maps/sonya-raber-balliet-family.svg) shows how Melissa and Raegan connect through Sonya. These are family-supplied links; the dates and marriage records are still open.

The family's long Berwick connection is supported by censuses, the 1915 county biography, Shirley's 2018 obituary and Paulene's account. Paulene recalled Mabel/Pearl's **1919 birth near Muncy**, and Justin says **Melissa and her husband Paul Kramer now live in the Muncy area**; the [place-sequence map](../context/family-place-sequences.md) distinguishes Muncy Valley from Muncy.
