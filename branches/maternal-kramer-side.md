# Justin's maternal side: a Berwick guide

![Two Berwick branches joining at Paul Miller and Sonya Raber, then Melissa Miller Kramer](../maps/miller-raber-family.svg)

Justin names **Paul Miller** and **Sonya Raber** as his mother **Melissa Miller Kramer's** parents. Melissa married **Paul Joseph Kramer**; Justin says she worked in Pennsylvania real estate in the 2000s. The [Miller page](miller-gower-reese.md) follows Paul's Berwick ancestors; the [Raber page](raber-kreisher-fairchild.md) holds Sonya's line and the still unnamed Fairchild ancestor. These recent links are **family supplied**. A published account by Paul's sister Paulene gives unusually rich detail on the older Miller household, but its dates and property story still need original records.

The family's long Berwick connection is now supported by **1910, 1920 and 1940 census indexes** plus Paulene's account. Paulene also recalled Mabel/Pearl's **1919 birth near Muncy**, and Justin says **Melissa and her husband Paul Kramer now live in the Muncy area**; the [place-sequence map](../context/family-place-sequences.md) shows why this is a regional return across generations while distinguishing Muncy Valley from Muncy. The Raber/Kreisher/Fairchild places are presently a family memory; each person still needs a record before we assign a particular address or job.
