# James Smith's possible Washington, DC, family

![Record trail from the 1917 Smith–Mulhall marriage through three censuses to the still-open Fairfax identity bridge](../maps/smith-dc-candidate-timeline.svg)

**The promising identification:** a [Social Security record](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:6K4N-S22V?lang=en) names **James Allen Smith**, born in Washington, DC, on **14 October 1933**, deceased **6 June 1990** with a Fairfax County residence. It gives parents **Raymond J. Smith** and **“Allice Maulhall.”** Alice Weatherford's [1986 marriage return](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:QK9V-J612?lang=en) names her father **James Allen Smith**, and his [shared marker](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/106660847/james_a-smith) says **1933–1990**. The agreement is strong, but none of these records *together* names Gladys as the Social Security James's wife. Keep the earlier generations **provisional** until a marriage, obituary or death record makes that bridge.

Its automated event place reads *Washington Township, Iowa*, inconsistent with the named Washington newspaper, so no locality is inferred from that field. |

The three census sheets and notice independently follow **one Raymond–Alice household** across decades, with son James in 1940 and 1950. Raymond reported optician work in both 1930 and 1950; this does not prove uninterrupted employment. The records show a possible route from James's DC childhood to the Fairfax County adult in the Social Security record. They do **not** document the move itself or establish that this was **Gladys Blevins Smith's husband**. The [Smith branch](smith.md) keeps Raymond and Alice as candidate ancestors for that reason.

**Best next check:** obtain James Allen Smith's **1990 obituary or death certificate** naming Gladys or another distinctive family member, or the couple's marriage return naming James's parents. Then examine Raymond and Alice's 1917 original marriage entry and earlier DC households before extending either surname further.
