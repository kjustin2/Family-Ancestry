# Smith line: the Miller Drive home

For Ashley's question about arrival in America, see the [immigration evidence guide](ashley-immigration.md). Alice's parents are now documented, but neither set of their parents nor an immigrant has been securely identified.

For **each known generation, birth/death status, and all supplied Smith siblings**, see the [Weatherford–Smith generation guide](weatherford-smith-generations.md).

**Documented parents:** Alice Marie Smith's [1986 Virginia marriage return, image 470](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-C9TY-595M-1?view=index&personArk=%2Fark%3A%2F61903%2F1%3A1%3AQK9V-J612&action=view&cc=2370234&lang=en) names her father **James Allen Smith** and her mother's maiden name **Gladys Louise Blevins**. It gives Alice's residence as **6330 Miller Drive, Fairfax County**, when she married John Gordon Weatherford on **12 April 1986**. She reported Maryland as her birth state and completing grade 12. The document supports the family's Prince George's County birthplace account, though it only says Maryland. The family recalls that James died in the 1990s; no death record has yet matched him.

**Sibling evidence:** A [1976 marriage return for Jane Ann Smith](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-C9TX-59CG-9?view=index&personArk=%2Fark%3A%2F61903%2F1%3A1%3AQVY5-GZKP&action=view&cc=2370234&lang=en) gives the same father and mother's maiden name and the same 6330 Miller Drive home. Jane married **Richard Henry Horesky** on **10 April 1976**. A [1983 marriage return](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-C9TT-NHKX-W?view=index&personArk=%2Fark%3A%2F61903%2F1%3A1%3AQV13-2NVL&action=view&cc=2370234&lang=en) names **James Howard Smith** with parents James Allen Smith and “Glaydis Louise Blevins.” He married Lynn Anne Barham on **1 October 1983**. The matching parent pair makes him another documented sibling. Justin identifies **James Howard** as the family member called **“Buck.”** Tim and Carolyn remain family-supplied siblings; no birth order is established. [Family account] · [marriage images above].

The family says Alice attended **Hayfield High School**; her marriage return confirms completion of grade 12, though it does not name the school. A yearbook or diploma would test attendance. The same return says John completed grade 12 and lived at **6312 Miller Drive** in 1986, tying the two known family homes to the couple at their marriage.

The [county record](https://icare.fairfaxcounty.gov/ffxcare/search/commonsearch.aspx?mode=address) for parcel **0913 06030003** dates the house to **1960**. A **1990 $0 title entry** names **“Gladys L Smith heirs of”** as buyer, and a **24 June 2010 valid sale for $360,000** names those heirs as sellers (deed **21105-0038**). This supports a Smith family property connection but does not identify the heirs, date Gladys's death, or show how sale proceeds were shared. A 1966 $20,250 entry lacks a named buyer and is not attributed to the family.
