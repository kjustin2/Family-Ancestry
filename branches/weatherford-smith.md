# Weatherford and Smith: Alexandria, Virginia

**Family-supplied starting point:** Ashley Marie Weatherford is Justin's wife. Ashley identifies her parents as **John Weatherford** and **Alice Smith**; all three are associated with Alexandria, Virginia. This pass found no record that uniquely identifies the correct John or Alice. Their names should not be joined to public search hits based only on a common name and city.

## What Alexandria can tell us now

The [City of Alexandria history timeline](https://alexandriava.gov/historic-alexandria/alexandria-history-a-timeline) and [historic maps](https://alexandriava.gov/historic-alexandria/basic-page/historic-alexandria-maps) show a Potomac port city with layers of federal, Civil War and modern Washington-area history. The city's [school-history investigation](https://alexandriava.gov/cultural-history/black-education-in-alexandria-a-legacy-of-triumph-and-struggle) documents segregated schools and the long process of desegregation. **None of this establishes which school a Weatherford or Smith attended, their race, their neighborhood, or their family's situation.**

The [Alexandria Library local-history guide](https://alexandriava.gov/historic-alexandria/conducting-your-own-historic-research) points to city directories, census microfilm, newspapers, deeds, wills, school records and photograph collections. These are the best next sources once an older ancestor's full name plus a year or address differentiates this family from namesakes.

## First records to add

1. A family-provided parent or grandparent obituary, marriage certificate, family Bible page, or one older full name with approximate years.
2. A city directory or census entry that matches **two** independent details, then birth/marriage/probate records for the prior generation.
3. School and neighborhood context only after a school or address is supported by a record.
