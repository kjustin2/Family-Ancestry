# When did Ashley's family arrive in America?

**We do not yet have a documented immigration date for any of Ashley's identified branches.** The Weatherford line now reaches **George C. Weatherford and Ella Lumpkin**, married in **Pittsylvania County, Virginia, on 2 July 1884**. Their son **Doctor Duffy Weatherford** was born in **Caswell County, North Carolina**, in 1890, according to his [1966 death certificate](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-C95D-9HD1?view=index&personArk=%2Fark%3A%2F61903%2F1%3A1%3AQVRZ-2C68&action=view&lang=en). This establishes family residence in the U.S. by 1884, **not** who immigrated or when. The [1884 marriage register](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QS7-99LX-MC9V?view=index&personArk=%2Fark%3A%2F61903%2F1%3A1%3A6NJ1-VGMH&action=view&lang=en) proposes one further generation, but a candidate 1880 census has conflicting parent names; see the [Weatherford evidence](weatherford.md#earlier-danville-and-north-carolina-generations).

An online [FamilySearch profile of a seventeenth-century William Weatherford](https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/MX36-GRZ/william-weatherford-ii-1645-1732) places a man of that surname in colonial Virginia. **No parent-child chain connects him to Ashley**, and a same-surname person in colonial Virginia would not establish when *her* line immigrated. The [Library of Virginia](https://www.lva.virginia.gov/public/guides/rn16_biographical.htm) explains that original Virginia land patents, grants, and other colonial records can be searched once a named ancestor is established.

**How to get a defensible date:** Reconcile George's parent names, then extend the **Weatherford, Lumpkin, Neathery and Phelps** lines through birth, marriage, death, census and church records until one person has an overseas birthplace. Corroborate that person with a passenger, naturalization or early land record. Separately identify **Florence Morrison's** parents and **James Allen Smith's and Gladys Louise Blevins's** parents. A surname's apparent national origin or a distant public tree is not a personal arrival record. A same-named [Gladys Blevins tree profile](https://www.familysearch.org/tree/person/details/G91H-5C5) has Alice's marriage attached but also a separate Morgan family; its proposed earlier generations need an independent bridge before being assigned to Ashley.
