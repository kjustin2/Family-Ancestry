# Cowan and Mason: Nora Hines's Virginia family

![Record-based Cowan and Mason connections from 1880 to 1900](../maps/cowan-mason-evidence.svg)

**The solid chain:** [Nora J. Cowan's 1892 birth-register page](../sources/records/nora-cowan-birth-register-1892.jpg), row 12, names **Jasper Cowan and Mary Cowan** as parents in Washington County, Virginia. Her [1937 death certificate](../sources/records/nora-jane-cowan-hines-death-1937.jpg) calls them **Jasper N. Cowan and Mary Ann Mason** and names husband **John J. Hines**. The [1900 original census](../sources/records/cowan-household-census-1900.jpg), Washington County sheet 4A, lines 44–50, puts **Nora J.** with Jasper N., Mary A., three younger children and Jasper's father **Isaac G.** These independent records make the family connection strong.

## Siblings named on contemporary household sheets

- **Nora's younger Cowan siblings in 1900:** **James H.** (about 6), **Robert** (about 4), and **Bertie K.** (about 2) appear with Nora, Jasper and Mary. The [public tree](https://www.familysearch.org/en/tree/person/details/LYRS-YF6) adds Virginia, Alice, Ellen, Lillian and Louis, and calls the 1900 youngest child *Pearl*. These later names and **Bertie/Pearl** identity need independent records before calling the nine-child list complete.
- **Jasper's siblings in the 1880 Isaac–Elizabeth household:** **Thomas H.** (17), **Margaret A.** (14), **Milton E.** (8), and **Sarah C.** (6). They are the siblings visible on that [census index](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:MCR8-PQ9?lang=en), not necessarily all the couple's children.
- **Mary's siblings in the 1880 Henry–Frances household:** **William** (12), **James** (6), **Owen** (4), and **Augustus** (3) appear with nine-year-old Mary in [Lee County](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:MC5F-5K2?lang=en). Their later lives have not been checked.

## Work and movement, with limits

The [1880 household](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:MCR8-PQ9?lang=en) lists twelve-year-old Jasper as a **farm laborer** in North Fork, Washington County. A [24 December 1889 marriage index](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:XR9J-SWG?lang=en) places a **Jasper C. Cowen, 22**, with **Mary E. Mason, 17**, in **Scott County**. A second [register index](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:ZVZD-HVPZ?lang=en) calls him *Jasper N.* and also names Mary E.; its year is missing in the index, and its original image is restricted to FamilySearch centers or affiliates. The middle initial difference and 1900 report of **12 years married** leave the exact date/identity worth checking, but names, spouse, ages, and neighboring counties strongly fit this couple.

By **1900**, the original [Kinderhook sheet](../sources/records/cowan-household-census-1900.jpg) calls Jasper a **farmer** and records his father Isaac in the home. His 1942 death certificate instead says his usual occupation was **mechanic**. That supports a change of work by later life; it does not say where he trained, how prosperous the farm was, or when he left it. Nora's later Hines household moved into Bristol's railroad economy; see the [Smith–Hines guide](smith.md#places-work-and-the-miller-drive-chapter). The [Virginia county maps and period views](../context/virginia-colonial-views.md) are place context, not depictions of this particular family.
