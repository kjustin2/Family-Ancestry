# Weatherford line: Danville to Franconia

**Working line:** **D.D. Weatherford** and **Annie Neatherly Weatherford** → **Garnett Burnell Weatherford Sr.** and **Florence Ruth Morrison Weatherford** → **John Gordon Weatherford** → **Ashley Marie Weatherford**. John's and Ashley's relationships come from the family and are corroborated in part by Garnett Sr.'s obituary. Garnett's parent link comes from the obituary of his sister Helen; see the evidence below. D.D.'s expanded given names are not established.

## Garnett and Florence

The family's account places John's parents at **6312 Miller Drive**, close to John and Alice's Colette Drive home. The [Fairfax County parcel history](https://icare.fairfaxcounty.gov/ffxcare/search/commonsearch.aspx?mode=address) names **Garnett B. Weatherford Sr.** as a **1980 $104,500 buyer** and **Florence Ruth Weatherford** in a 2013 title entry and a verified **2019 $645,000 sale**. These are nominal property transactions, with no evidence yet of debt or proceeds distribution.

A [Fairfax County marriage-license index](https://www.fairfaxcounty.gov/circuit/sites/circuit/files/assets/documents/pdf/hrc/marriage-book-index-1853-1957-brides.pdf) pairs **Florence Ruth Morrison** with **“Garrett Burnell Weatherford”** on **16 January 1954**, license **ML0358**, book **37**. The groom's first name conflicts with **Garnett** in the parcel record and two obituaries. The shared middle name, unusual spouse name and 1954 timing make this a strong match, but the original license should settle the spelling and whether the index date was the license or ceremony date. The [Washington Post obituary](https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/local/2007/07/03/obituaries/43ba782a-181c-4c59-a78e-7d361d86bf4a/) says they were married for 53 years by 2007.

Garnett's [Washington Post profile](https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/local/2007/07/03/obituaries/43ba782a-181c-4c59-a78e-7d361d86bf4a/) says he was born in **Danville, Virginia**, served in the **Navy in the Pacific during World War II**, drove for **Alexandria, Barcroft and Washington (AB&W)** transit for 25 years, then **Metro** for 16 years after Metro took over AB&W in 1973. He died **27 June 2007**, aged 80. This is a rare occupation story tied to an institution we can picture: the [City of Alexandria's AB&W history and 1960s bus image](https://media.alexandriava.gov/docs-archives/historic/info/attic/2010/attic20101021bus.pdf) shows what the fleet looked like, while [WMATA confirms the 1973 takeover](https://www.wmata.com/news/metrobus-50th-annivesary.html). The picture is of a company bus, not a verified bus Garnett personally drove.

The family says Florence worked at **Burke & Herbert Bank**. Her role and dates remain unverified; the [bank's own history](https://www.burkeandherbertbank.com/were-celebrating-169-years-of-being-at-your-service/) says it opened in Alexandria in 1852. Garnett's [published death notice](https://www.legacy.com/us/obituaries/washingtonpost/name/garnett-weatherford-obituary?id=2252863) calls him **“Gee Bee”** and names Florence, children **Garnett “Bee” Jr., Stephen W., Elaine M. and John G. Weatherford Sr.** Garnett Jr.'s [own notice](https://www.legacy.com/person/garnett-%28bee%29-b.-weatherford-jr.-5409122) says he died **31 October 2003**; the father's later *Post* profile says **2004**, an apparent error. John's family separately confirms these siblings.

## Earlier Danville generation

The [2007 obituary of Helen Weatherford Barksdale](https://www.legacy.com/us/obituaries/godanriver/name/helen-barksdale-obituary?id=33333924) names her parents **D.D. Weatherford** and **Annie Neatherly Weatherford**, then lists **Garnett Weatherford of Alexandria** among her brothers. Garnett's own 2007 notice names **Thelma Snead, Betty Atkinson and D.D. Weatherford Jr.** as siblings, matching Helen's list. Taken together, the two obituaries strongly connect Garnett Sr. to D.D. and Annie; the original birth or marriage record remains the direct test. Helen's obituary also places this family around Danville and neighboring Pittsylvania County, where she attended **Dan River High School**. That was **Helen's** school, not a known school for Garnett.

A [derivative Southside family compilation](https://cottagehill.com/southside/f544.htm) expands D.D. to **Doctor Duffy Weatherford Sr.** and Annie to **Annie Lee Neatherly**. This is a **proposed spelling**, not yet verified from an original record. It should guide searches, not silently replace the obituary initials.
