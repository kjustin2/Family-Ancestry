# The Andrews and Kennedy household on Flick Street

**The short version:** The [1920 census, first sheet](../sources/records/frank-andrews-family-census-1920-sheet-3a.jpg) and [continuation](../sources/records/frank-andrews-family-census-1920-sheet-3b.jpg) show Frank and Mary Andrews with five children. Mary's [1929 death certificate](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/126529268/mary-andrews/photo#view-photo=218591682) names her parents **Edward Kennedy and Mary Durkin** and records **21 Flick Street**. The [1930 census](../sources/records/frank-andrews-children-census-1930.jpg) shows Frank widowed with six children, including **Mary and Rita as sisters**. This corrects Rita's misleading “daughter” label in the [1940 Kramer household](../sources/records/ferdinand-mary-kramer-census-1940-continuation.jpg). Younger Mary was Justin Kramer's great-grandmother through her son Fred.

The [original newspaper clipping](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/126529268/mary-andrews/photo#view-photo=218591721), identified by the memorial as *Wilkes-Barre Record*, 27 May, calls it an illness of complications and names **Anna, Edward, Francis, Michael, Mary and Rita**, four Kennedy brothers and two married sisters. | Two contemporary sources independently say **21 Flick**, so that number is real evidence rather than a memorial transcription alone. The report of illness should not be expanded into a modern diagnosis or an assumed reason for the later change of address. [A photographed shared Andrews marker](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/126529268/mary-andrews/photo#view-photo=97953087) reads Mary **1887–1929** and Frank **1887–1965**. |
| **17 Apr 1930** | The [original census](../sources/records/frank-andrews-children-census-1930.jpg), Wilkes-Barre Ward 17, ED 40-270, sheet 11A, lines **25–31**, places widowed **Frank, 43**, at **71 Flick Street** with six children: **Anna, 20; Edward, 18; Francis, 17; Michael, 15; Mary, 13; Rita, 7**. The $5,000 is a reported property value, **not family net worth**, and the census does not name the children's mother. Frank said he was born in New York and both parents in Poland. |
| **1936–40** | Mary gave **71 Flick** on her [1936 signed marriage form](../sources/records/ferdinand-louis-kramer-mary-andrews-marriage-1936.jpg), which names **Frank Andrews and Mary Kennedy** as her parents. By [1940](../sources/records/ferdinand-mary-kramer-census-1940.jpg), she and Ferdinand Louis rented at **19 Flick** for **$5/month** with their infant son and Rita. | The 1940 “daughter” entry for Rita conflicts with the **1930 father-and-daughters household**; she was Mary's sister. Two recorded house numbers do not by themselves prove a physical move, sale, inheritance, or why Rita stayed with Mary. |
| **1948** | The [Bell telephone directory, printed p. 85 (PDF p. 87)](https://westpittstonhistory.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/1948-Bell-Telephone-Directory-Pittston-_-Nearby-Points.pdf#page=87) lists **“Andrews Frank E r 19 Flick”**. It does not prove he stayed there continuously or that the current photographed structure dates from his lifetime. |

**From Ireland to the Parsons coal district:** Edward Kennedy and **Mary Durkin Kennedy** are named as Mary Andrews's parents on her [1929 certificate](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/126529268/mary-andrews/photo#view-photo=218591682). The [1900 household](../sources/records/edward-mary-kennedy-census-1900-sheet-22a.jpg) puts the adults' birthplaces in **Ireland**, their children in **Pennsylvania**, and Edward at a **coal mine**. Its Mary A. is strongly matched to the later Mary Andrews by parents, locality and the brothers in her [original 1929 notice](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/126529268/mary-andrews/photo#view-photo=218591721). The reported early-1880s arrivals give **no Irish county, ship or motive**. The notice names brothers **John, Thomas, Henry and Michael Kennedy**, plus two married sisters, **Mrs. Joseph Dooley** and **Mrs. Thomas McCarthy of New York City**. The 1900 sheet also lists Edward, Margaret and Patrick; the sisters' given names and later histories need checking. [Anna's](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:6K95-L1NX?lang=en) and [Francis's](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:6K9P-3M8R?lang=en) Social Security indexes each name **Frank Andrews and Mary Kennedy** as parents. Rita's own parent-naming birth record remains open.

![Kennedy parents reported Ireland as birthplace and Parsons as their 1900 home; the voyage details are unknown](../maps/kennedy-ireland-parsons.svg)

**Three Flick Street numbers need a property check:** the **1929 certificate and original clipping both say 21**; the 1930 census and 1936 marriage form say **71**; the 1940 Kramer rental, 1948 directory, 1950 census and 1976 obituary say **19**. These might be moves or renumbering; no deed, building card or historic street map has settled that. Luzerne County's [current structure-address layer](https://gis.luzernecounty.org/server/rest/services/PublicStreetsStructures/MapServer/0) contains **19**, but a search returned no **21 or 71 Flick** point. That is a present database gap, not proof those older houses were demolished. [Open the map for 21 Flick](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=21+Flick+St+Wilkes-Barre+PA), but do not label any present building as the 1929 home yet.
